From road footage
to useful insight.

A practical concept connecting vehicle-mounted sensing, location data, and a clear online dashboard to help teams understand road conditions.

◉

Camera-assisted detection

Use road-facing camera footage with computer-vision software to identify potential potholes and surface defects.

⌖

GPS location tagging

Attach coordinates and timestamps to detections so each reported issue can be located and reviewed.

▥

One clear dashboard

Organize reports, review detections, and visualize road issues in one place as the system develops.

The process

A simple path from detection to action.

STEP 01

Capture

A camera mounted on a vehicle records the road ahead.

STEP 02

Detect

Detection software analyzes frames and flags possible potholes.

STEP 03

Locate

GPS adds coordinates and time to each confirmed report.

STEP 04

Visualize

Reports can be uploaded and shown on the online dashboard.

Designed for visibility

Every report.
One overview.

The future RoadSense360 dashboard can bring field detections into a single workspace, helping users review locations, prioritize inspections, and follow issue status.

  • Location-based pothole reports
  • Detection time and review status
  • Map view and report history
  • Room to add analytics and alerts
